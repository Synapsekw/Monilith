Keystone / favicon · mark: Cleave

The cleaved monolith.

The mark is a monolith with a thin slice sheared off the top, a diagonal of light in the crack. It carries the brand story rather than just repeating the wordmark. Below full size, the crack gets a bolder cut so it survives a 16 px tab.

Two cuts, one mark

Refined vs 16pxthe crack scales with the tile
REFINED32 px and up
16 PX CUTwider crack, bolder pieces
The refined cut keeps a fine 5-unit crack for crisp screens. The 16 px cut widens the gap and thickens both pieces so the split still reads when the whole mark is only 16 px tall.
