The premise

Most teams do not have a tooling problem. They have a seams problem: work in one app, the plan in a second, the numbers in a third, and a day lost each week reconciling all three.

MONOLITH removes the seams. Every surface reads from the same records, so nothing has to be copied, synced, or explained twice.

Everything your team opened in a different app, now under one roof.

Each capability shares one data model, one permission scheme, and one history. This is the shortlist it retires.
