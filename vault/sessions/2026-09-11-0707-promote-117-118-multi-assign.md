## How to test (for the user)

1. On www.monolith.works, open a board with a People column and click a People cell. The member list opens.
2. Click one member: their avatar and name appear in the cell and the list stays open.
3. Click a second member: both are assigned without reopening the cell.
4. Click an assigned member again: they are removed; the list stays open.
5. Press Escape or click outside: the list closes. Reload; both picks persist.
6. A Status cell still closes the moment you pick a value. A multi-select Dropdown cell behaves like People.
7. The perf-pass and board-view-prefs walkthroughs are in their own session notes and also apply to production now.
