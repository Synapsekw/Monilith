Motion What every variant shares

  • Fold: width eases 300 → 48/0/56px over 360ms on the Keystone curve (cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)). The open content exits first (140ms fade + 24px slide right), then the collapsed form fades in behind it.
  • Unfold: width first; the header tabs, title and turns slide in from the right with a 60ms stagger (turn 1 at 120ms, turn 2 at 180ms…), the composer rises last from 10px below. Nothing scales — Keystone moves on one axis.
  • Presence: the active agent's dot pulses (ring expands 6px, 1.4s) while a run is live, in every state including collapsed — so a folded dock still tells you something is happening.
  • Reduced motion: the global prefers-reduced-motion rule in globals.css collapses all of this to instant; nothing here re-implements it.
  • Real build: width comes from the existing use-dock-state (persisted, resizable); the fold is a CSS transition on the same value, and the two layers are one component with data-open — no Framer needed.
  • Mix freely: e.g. V1's open finish with V2's collapse-into-header, or V3's spine with V1's plainer transcript.
